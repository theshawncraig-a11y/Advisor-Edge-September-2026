Respond. Prospect.
Follow Up.

Walk your client through these three priorities with their team. Start with the leads asking for contact, then work prospecting and reminders so agents know where to focus each day.

Coach agents to check Contact Requested first and respond while the lead is actively asking to connect.

Help clients establish a prospecting order: new leads first, attempted contacts next, then recently re-engaged leads.

Encourage agents to work every due and overdue reminder so follow-up stays consistent after the first conversation.

Receive Pictures From Leads

MMS support, now live in the desktop CRM.

Leads can now text images, GIFs, HEIC files, and PDFs to an agent’s CINC number, and they display directly inside the CRM instead of arriving as broken or unreadable attachments. No more asking a lead to resend a photo to a personal cell — if a lead texts a yard sign or a screenshot of a listing, the agent can see it immediately, right where the rest of the conversation lives.

Available Now Desktop CRM · Receive-only
How it works: fully automatic.

CINC receives the MMS and displays it in the CRM — no setup or action needed from the agent.

Where Agents Can View Images
01 Lead Profile → Communications

Inline in the text thread — click any image to enlarge.

02 Lead Profile → Activity

Logged alongside every other piece of lead activity.

03 Communications → Agent Inbox

From the main CINC navigation — no need to open the lead profile.

Scope & Limits
Formats JPEG · PNG · GIF · HEIC · PDF
Size limit 5 MB combined Message + attachment. Carriers may impose additional limits.
Retention 13 months Viewable in the CINC CRM.

Coming soon: not yet available in the CINC Agent App or an agent’s native phone texting app.

Receive-only: agents can’t yet send images to leads through CINC — property sharing remains the workaround for outbound.
